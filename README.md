Employee Performance Dashboard
Project Overview
The Employee Performance Dashboard is an Excel-based analytics project designed to help explore employee performance across departments and locations. It brings key performance indicators and operational metrics into a clear, visual format to support data-informed review and decision-making.
Business Objectives
- Monitor employee performance and productivity.
- Compare target tasks with completed tasks.
- Review work quality, attendance, and customer satisfaction.
- Explore revenue by employee and location.
- Compare performance across departments and over time.
- Identify patterns that may warrant further investigation.
Dataset
The workbook contains 1,000 records covering six employees, three departments, and three locations. The stated date range is January 2024 through August 2026.

The dataset includes fields related to:
- Employee, department, and location
- Date
- Target and completed tasks
- Quality scores
- Attendance rates
- Customer satisfaction
- Revenue (AED)
- Performance scores
Before publishing, verify these details against the final workbook and its data dictionary.

Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel

Analysis Questions
1. How does employee performance change over time?
2. How do target tasks compare with completed tasks?
3. Which employees have the highest performance scores?
4. How does performance vary across departments?
5. What do quality scores, attendance rates, and customer satisfaction indicate?
6. How is revenue distributed by employee and location?
7. Which patterns or areas may need further investigation?
